Classification Comparison Report Summary

Task B Overview:
A classification performance comparison was conducted using three CNN-based architectures—VGG16_BN, ResNet50, and EfficientNet_B3—on the Pascal VOC 2008 dataset. Each model was trained for 10 epochs using a batch size of 32 on a CUDA-enabled device.

---

Training and Evaluation Summary:

| Model           | mAP    | F1 Score (Weighted) | Accuracy (Exact Match) |
|----------------|--------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| VGG16_BN        | 0.2755 | 0.3904              | 0.2719                  |
| ResNet50        | 0.5837 | 0.6379              | 0.4412                  |
| EfficientNet_B3 | 0.7082 | 0.7555              | 0.5597                  |

- VGG16_BN showed the lowest performance across all metrics, with an exact match accuracy of 27.19%.
- ResNet50 demonstrated significant improvement, achieving an accuracy of 44.12% and a weighted F1 score of 0.6379.
- EfficientNet_B3 outperformed both models with the highest accuracy (55.97%), weighted F1 score (0.7555), and mean average precision (mAP) of 0.7082.

---

Training Efficiency:
- All models completed training without batch skips.
- EfficientNet_B3 had the fastest training per epoch (~24s), while VGG16_BN was the slowest (~44s per epoch average).

---

Post-Training Visualizations:
Top 10 ranked images per class were generated for all 20 VOC categories (e.g., aeroplane, cat, person, etc.) across all three models to aid qualitative analysis.

---

Conclusion:
EfficientNet_B3 is the most effective model in this comparison, delivering superior classification performance across all evaluation metrics. This makes it the preferred architecture for deployment in similar multi-class classification tasks.
